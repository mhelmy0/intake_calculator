Operational Notes

  • Maintain V ≥ 0.6 m/s in single-pipe operation to prevent sediment deposition.
  • Keep both pipes balanced via butterfly valve modulation; isolate one pipe for screen cleaning during off-peak.
  • Monitor differential pressure across coarse screens — replace/clean when ΔP exceeds 50% of clean value.
  • Verify air-release valves at high points are leak-free; trapped air increases hf and risks vortex.
  • Surge analysis must be repeated whenever closure time, valve type, or pipe schedule changes.
